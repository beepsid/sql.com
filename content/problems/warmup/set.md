**Start here if you're new.** One table, `movies`, and questions that only need the beginner topics: SELECT, WHERE, AND / OR, NULL, ORDER BY, LIMIT, DISTINCT, COUNT and CASE. Every question links to the topic that teaches it.

- `rating` is out of 10, and NULL when a movie hasn't been rated yet
- `minutes` is how long the movie is
- row order doesn't matter, unless the question asks for the "top" or "newest" few

Your answer is checked on the sample movies you can see, plus a few hidden ones, so don't hardcode titles.
